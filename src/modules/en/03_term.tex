\section*{3. Lease Term}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
    \item This contract is concluded for a definite period. The lease begins on \Rentstartdate \ and ends on \Rentenddate. The lease period can be extended by a written amendment to this contract.
    \item Before the expiry of the agreed lease term, the rental relationship can be terminated by mutual written agreement between the Landlord and the Tenant, or by written notice.
    \item The written notice must state the period when the lease is to end. The notice period must not be less than three months and must end at the end of a calendar month. The notice period shall start on the first day of the month following the month in which the notice was delivered to the other party.
\end{enumerate}
