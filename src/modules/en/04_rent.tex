\section*{4. Rent and Other Payments}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
    \item The Tenant undertakes to pay the Landlord for each calendar month of the lease the rent in the amount of \Rentrentamount,- CZK and advances for services associated with the use of the apartment in the amount of \Rentservicesamount,- CZK, meaning a total of \Renttotalamount,- CZK, always strictly by the \Rentpaymentday day of the paid month to the account: \Rentbankaccount.
    \item The services associated with the use of the apartment include electricity, heat, and hot water supply, water and sewage, energy supply to common areas, and costs associated with the administration, cleaning, and maintenance of the common areas, and garbage.
    \item Telecom services in the apartment will be paid by the Tenant directly to the providers of these services.
    \item The settlement of the advances for the services will take place after the settlement has been carried out for the previous calendar year by the respective property manager. Any underpayment/overpayment will be settled by the contracting parties within 30 days after the presentation of a written bill by the Landlord.
\end{enumerate}
