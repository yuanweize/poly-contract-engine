\section*{7. Rights and Obligations of the Parties}

\textbf{The Landlord is obliged to:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item hand over the apartment to the Tenant in a condition fit for proper use, maintain it in this condition at their own expense, and ensure the Tenant’s full and undisturbed exercise of rights associated with the use of the apartment,
    \item perform repairs that are not minor repairs without undue delay.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{The Landlord has the right to:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item carry out construction adjustments and other essential changes to the apartment only with the Tenant's consent,
    \item perform minor repairs and routine maintenance at their own expense if the Tenant fails to do so in a timely manner, and subsequently demand compensation,
    \item remove at their own expense any damage caused by the Tenant and demand compensation.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{The Tenant is obliged to:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item use the apartment exclusively for housing purposes,
    \item ensure that no damage occurs to the apartment,
    \item keep the apartment in a clean and usable condition at their own expense, and perform minor repairs (up to 1,000 CZK),
    \item maintain electrical appliances not part of the apartment in operable condition,
    \item allow the Landlord access to the apartment for inspection purposes,
    \item not sublease the apartment to a third party,
    \item not smoke in the apartment.
\end{itemize}
