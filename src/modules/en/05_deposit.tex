\section*{5. Lease Security}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
    \item Upon signing this contract, the Tenant has transferred an interest-free security deposit in the amount of \DepositAmount,- CZK to the Landlord. Should the Tenant default on performing their pecuniary obligations under this Contract towards the Landlord, the Landlord is entitled to unilaterally offset their claims against the deposit. The Tenant is then obligated to replenish the deposit to the full amount within 15 days after presentation of a written demand.
    \item The aforementioned deposit, or its unutilized part, will be returned to the Tenant after the end of the lease following a final settlement of rent and services, provided that all financial claims of the Landlord are fully paid.
\end{enumerate}
