\section*{6. Handover of the Apartment}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
    \item The Landlord undertakes to hand over the apartment (including all necessary keys) to the Tenant no later than on the day the lease starts. A protocol on the handover will be drawn up, confirming the state of the equipment and fittings, as well as the states of the water meter, electricity meter, and gas meter.
    \item The Tenant undertakes to vacate and hand over the apartment to the Landlord no later than on the day the lease ends, in the state in which they took over the apartment, taking into account usual wear and tear. A handover protocol will be drawn up recording the meter states and the new contact details of the Tenant. The Tenant also commits to canceling their permanent residence registration from the given address by this date.
\end{enumerate}
